# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Mithaq community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, empathetic, and constructive.
- Respect privacy and cultural or religious differences.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.
- Never expose or solicit private member information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **conduct@example.com**. Replace this placeholder with a monitored project address before public launch. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the community or its members.
